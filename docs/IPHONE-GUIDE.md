# Md. Robiul Islam's card on iPhone: step-by-step guide

Card address: **https://alshams8.github.io/Business-Card-Md-Robiul-Islam/**
(Keep the capital letters. The NFC tag, the QR code and the contact file all point here.)

## Quick answer: what works iPhone to iPhone

| Way to share | Needs | What the other person gets |
|---|---|---|
| **NameDrop** (hold the iPhones together) | Both iPhones on iOS 17.1 or later | Name, the phone numbers and emails you choose, photo / Contact Poster |
| **AirDrop the full contact** | AirDrop on, nearby iPhone | Everything: both numbers, both emails, photo, title, company, all three office addresses, website, card link |
| **AirDrop or send the card link** | Any phone | The live digital card (call, WhatsApp, WeChat, save contact, map) |
| **QR code** | Any phone camera | The live digital card |
| **NFC tag** (sticker or card) | iPhone XS or newer, or Android with NFC | The live digital card |

An iPhone cannot act as an NFC tag itself, and a web page cannot send by NFC, so "tap my phone"
on iPhone means **NameDrop**, and "tap my card" means a cheap **NFC tag**.

## 1. Save the card and make it Mr. Robiul's own "My Card" (once, 3 minutes)

1. On Mr. Robiul's iPhone, open **https://alshams8.github.io/Business-Card-Md-Robiul-Islam/** in Safari.
2. Tap **Save Contact**, then **Create New Contact**, then **Done**.
3. Open **Settings › Apps › Contacts › My Info** (on iOS 17: **Settings › Contacts › My Info**) and choose **Md. Robiul Islam**.
4. Open the **Contacts** app, tap **My Card** at the top, then **Contact Photo & Poster** to set up the photo and poster other iPhones will see.
5. Check NameDrop is on: **Settings › General › AirDrop › Bringing Devices Together** must be on.

## 2. Share iPhone to iPhone with NameDrop

1. Both iPhones unlocked, on iOS 17.1 or later.
2. Hold the **top** of Mr. Robiul's iPhone a few centimetres from the top of the other iPhone.
3. When NameDrop appears, tap **Share** (swap cards) or **Receive Only**.
4. First time only: tap the arrow next to his name to pick which numbers and emails to share (choose both numbers and robiul.islam@bgols.com), then **Save**.

NameDrop sends only the name, chosen numbers or emails, and the Contact Poster. To give the
**full** card (office addresses, website, digital card link), use step 3 as well.

## 3. Send the full contact or the card link by AirDrop

- **Full contact:** Contacts › **My Card** › **Share Contact** › **AirDrop** › tap the other iPhone.
- **Live card link:** open the card in Safari › **Share Profile** › **Share via…** › **AirDrop**.
  With the card open on screen, you can also just hold the two iPhones together to AirDrop it (iOS 17+).
- The other iPhone must have AirDrop set to **Contacts Only** or **Everyone for 10 Minutes**
  (Control Centre, press and hold the network box, then AirDrop).

## 4. QR code, one tap away

1. Open the card in Safari, tap the **Share** button in Safari, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
   The card now opens like an app, also offline.
2. Optional, fastest: show the QR without unlocking menus.
   - Open the **Shortcuts** app › **+** › **Add Action** › **Open URLs** › enter **https://alshams8.github.io/Business-Card-Md-Robiul-Islam/#qr** › name it "My QR".
   - Then **Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap › Double Tap** › choose **My QR**.
     Now a double tap on the back of the iPhone opens the card already flipped to the QR side.
   - iPhone 15 Pro or newer: you can put the same shortcut on the **Action button** instead
     (Settings › Action Button › Shortcut).

## 5. NFC tag for anyone (iPhone or Android)

1. Buy blank **NTAG215** cards or **NTAG213** stickers (any phone accessory shop or online).
2. On the iPhone, install **NFC Tools** (by wakdev) from the App Store.
3. In NFC Tools: **Write › Add a record › URL / URI** › paste **https://alshams8.github.io/Business-Card-Md-Robiul-Islam/** › **OK** › **Write**,
   then hold the **top edge** of the iPhone against the tag.
4. Test: tap the tag with another phone. A banner appears; tap it to open the card.
5. When it works, lock it: NFC Tools › **Other › Lock tag** (permanent).

Tip: put the sticker on the back of his phone case. Then other people tap **his case** with their phone.

## 6. Optional: Apple Wallet pass

A Wallet pass with the QR code is possible, but every pass must be signed with a certificate from the
**Apple Developer Program** (USD 99 per year, company or personal account). If B-GOLS ever joins,
a Wallet pass for each staff card can be added. Until then, the Home Screen icon and Back Tap shortcut
do the same job for free.

## Troubleshooting

- **NameDrop does not appear:** both iPhones need iOS 17.1+, unlocked, and "Bringing Devices Together" on. Hold them top to top, really close.
- **Card shows an old version:** close and reopen it; updates arrive on the next open.
- **NFC tag does nothing:** the iPhone must be unlocked with the screen on; iPhone X and older need the NFC reader in Control Centre.

Source for NameDrop details: Apple Support, "Secure NameDrop", https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/secure-namedrop-ips97e16d3b1/web
